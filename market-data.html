AI Agent Authorization and Identity Pricing

An agent that can act without a permission boundary is not a feature. It is an incident waiting for a changelog entry.

Workforce identity: priced per seat

An AI agent needs the same thing a new hire needs — a bounded set of permissions, provable in an audit — except agents multiply faster than headcount and never sleep. The pricing model for workforce identity was built for humans and is now being stretched over agents: a per-user monthly fee, an annual contract, and a minimum that punishes small teams. Okta's published workforce pricing starts at $6 per user per month for Starter and $17 for Essentials, with a $1,500 annual minimum. Microsoft's Entra ID Premium P1 runs about $6 per user per month and P2 about $9 — and for organizations already paying for Microsoft 365, the marginal cost of core identity is often effectively zero, since it rides along with a subscription they already buy.

Customer identity: priced per active user

Customer identity inverts the model. Auth0, Okta's customer-identity cloud, charges per monthly active user rather than per seat: a free tier up to 25,000 MAUs, Essentials at $35 per month for 500 MAUs on the B2C plan ($150 for B2B), and Professional at $240 per month for 1,000 MAUs ($800 for B2B). That curve rewards apps with large registered-but-inactive bases and punishes apps whose users show up every day — the opposite shape from workforce pricing, and the reason an agent platform that authenticates both employees and end users usually ends up paying under two models at once.

The connection tax

For teams adding enterprise single sign-on to a product, the unit is neither a seat nor an active user but a connection. WorkOS charges $125 per connection per month for its first 15 connections, and SSOJet Business from $118 per month (2 connections, $59 per connection billed annually). The agent-entitlement question sits one layer above all three models: whatever the billing unit, the product has to answer what an agent may do, what it may spend, and what it may never touch — and to prove the answer afterwards. Vendors that price only authentication will eventually be asked to price authorization, and the ones that already do will charge for it.
